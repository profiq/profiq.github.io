THE QUADBLOOMFour specimens converge

How to Play

Cultivate a colony of microscopic cells until every one has bloomed.

The one rule

Press a cell and drag to another. They become opposite corners of a rectangle. The move is legal only when it spans at least 2 rows and 2 columns and all four corner cells currently share the same color. Interior cells can be anything.

On a legal release

  • Every unconverted cell inside becomes a Bloomform — permanently.
  • Every cell inside, Bloomforms included, evolves to a new color.
  • New colors immediately change which corners match next.
  • Bloomforms can be recolored again and again, and reused as corners.

Colors evolve, they don't cycle

Each cell's next color is drawn from the culture's own deterministic chemistry — it depends on where the cell sits, its current color and how many legal moves you have made. With three or more colors available it is always a different color, but never a predictable march around the palette. While a legal rectangle is drawn, every enclosed cell shows exactly the color it will become.

Culture Stability

  • Stability drains only while you are actively playing. It freezes in menus, help, pauses, summaries and when the tab is hidden.
  • Every freshly converted cell restores a little stability — big first-time conversions buy real breathing room.
  • A recolor-only move (everything inside is already bloomed) costs you time and restores nothing. Setup has a price.
  • At zero you enter Last Chance: stability freezes and you get exactly one final legal move. Illegal drags cost nothing. If that move blooms the last cells, it is a Miracle Bloom and the stage is won.

Scoring

  • Points only for newly converted cells — already-bloomed cells never score again.
  • A modest size bonus when a move converts at least one fresh cell.
  • Recolor-only moves score 0 but still count as a move. Illegal drags cost nothing at all.
  • Each stage ends with a bounded efficiency bonus comparing your move count to the culture's known solution.

Controls & restarting

  • Mouse, touch or pen: press, drag, release. Release outside the grid cancels.
  • Keyboard: arrows move the cursor, Space anchors then confirms, Esc cancels.
  • Restart Stage rebuilds the identical board and rewinds your score, moves, time and stability to the moment you entered the stage — there is nothing to farm.
  • Esc closes this and other overlays.
